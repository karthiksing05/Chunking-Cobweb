# Round 211: the gram >= .95 operating point at n=1000, strict judges

Code: `experiments/v3/diag/r211_collect_judge.py` (collect every n=1000 dump and judge it), `r211_analyse.py` (curves, operating points, paired tests), `r211_inventory.py`; edge low-temperature grids `logs/v3_r211_grid.sh` (w5 and w7, t0.3 0.5 0.7 1.0) and `logs/v3_r211b_grid.sh` (t0.1 0.15 0.2). Outputs in `r211/`: `r211_curves.tsv` (all curves), `r211_at95_gram.md` and `r211_at95_gt.md` (the full generated tables, which are also appended below), `r211_sets.json` (per-sentence flags), `r211_scores.json` (judge probabilities).

## Setup

* Judges: Qwen3-8B strict (real-sentence ceiling .958; scrambled false-pass .058, content-swapped .060) and phi-4 strict (ceiling .970; .068, .075), exactly the r209 prompt (16 shots). Llama strict is not used. The judging code caches the shared prefix of the prompt and scores only the sentence suffix; on 300 sentences re-judged from r209 it agrees on 99.7% (Qwen3) and 98.7% (phi-4) of decisions (mean |dp| .004), the residual being bf16 batching noise. Sentences already scored in r209 reuse the r209 scores.
* Dumps: every n=1000 dump with seeds 13/17/19/23 in the frontier files, excluding the "both-sides", parse and close variants and r210. Families: plain TRELLIS w2-w7, TRELLIS edge (edge_exact) w5/w6/w7/w8, LSTM, GPT, n-gram 3/4/5 samplers. Attempted draws per seed A = 1600/1544/1460/1476 (as r205).
* New generation: edge w5 and w7 at t0.1, 0.15, 0.2, 0.3, 0.5, 0.7, 1.0 (jobs 4789413-20 and 4790119-26), all four seeds. t0.1 produces no new sentence at all (every draw is a stored sentence), t0.15 and t0.2 produce 1-4 distinct new sentences per seed; those settings have no usable gram estimate and are left out of the interpolation (rule: mean distinct new per seed >= 20; the curves list them with "not used").
* Definitions as in r204/r209: gram = share of distinct new sentences judged grammatical (P(yes) > .5); tri|d = share of distinct new with an unstored trigram; dist = distinct new per attempted draw; fair = distinct new, grammatical, unstored-trigram sentences per attempted draw; gram|tri = gram among distinct new with an unstored trigram.
* Operating point: curves are piecewise linear in temperature through the measured settings (mean over seeds), no extrapolation. Candidate points = measured settings with gram >= threshold plus the interpolated crossings; the maximum of tri|d (or fair) over them is reported. Constraint thresholds: .95, .90, .95 x the judge's ceiling (.910 Qwen3, .921 phi-4), plus .85/.80/.75/.70 to show where the comparison becomes possible. A second set of tables constrains gram|tri instead of gram (the stricter reading for novelty): `gt` section at the end.

## Verdict

1. At gram >= .95 (and at .95 x ceiling for edge) no generator has an operating point under Qwen3-8B strict or phi-4 strict; the best usable grams are .91-.93 (LSTM, plain w3, n-gram) and they come with almost no novelty (fair <= .066 per draw, n-grams 0). TRELLIS edge tops out at .81 (t0.3, fair .007) before collapsing into stored sentences.
2. At the levels that exist (.90 and .95 x ceiling), the more novel and more productive point is the LSTM's (tri|d .44-.48, fair .034-.066); TRELLIS plain w3 reaches it with tri|d .16-.19 and fair .043-.056 and mostly by recombining stored trigrams; GPT and edge do not reach it.
3. TRELLIS does not produce more novel grammatical sentences per draw than the LSTM at any constraint from .70 to .90 on held-out seeds (0/2 wins at every constraint, both judges, on fair; 0/2 on tri|d except a tie at .70). Versus GPT it is ahead at .80-.85 (w3, 2/2 on 19/23, +.006 to +.049) where GPT must sit at t0.2-0.3 and where the difference is again a low-novelty regime, behind at .70-.75.
4. The r205 result (edge ties the LSTM at the fair optimum, gram .58-.66) therefore does not extend to the high-grammaticality end: the TRELLIS advantage vs GPT at .80-.85 is the only positive result, and its grammatical output is 71-87% stored-trigram recombination.

Caveats: the temperature grids differ (plain TRELLIS w3-w5 lowest t0.5, w6/w7 lowest t1.3; LSTM/GPT down to t0.15), so for plain w6/w7 "not reached" means "not measured below t1.3"; their gram at the lowest measured point is .50-.51 and their t0.5 points do not exist at n=1000 (no extrapolation). Settings below 20 distinct new sentences per seed are left out. Gram estimates at t <= 0.3 rest on 30-100 distinct sentences per seed per setting and the judges disagree on about 20% of borderline sentences (kappa .6, r209).
